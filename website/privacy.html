Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what the EggVyapar Android app ("the app") does with information. The app is developed by Vinayak Metagoli ("we", "the developer"). In short: your business data stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

1. Information the app stores

You enter your business information into the app yourself, for example your shop name, customers and suppliers (names, phone numbers, addresses), sales, purchases, payments, dues, stock, rates and expenses. This information is stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database, and is protected by your app PIN or fingerprint. It is not sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive in Backup & restore, you sign in with your Google account through Google, and the app asks for one permission:

  • See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).

The app uses this permission only to save, list, download and delete its own encrypted backup files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive, so that you can restore your data on a new or reset phone. The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Google Drive files. The backups are encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded. They are stored in your Google account, not with the developer, and the developer has no access to them.

The app does not read your Google contacts, email, profile or any other Google data. You can disconnect at any time in the app, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the hidden backups in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → EggVyapar → Delete hidden app data.

EggVyapar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Backup files on your phone and sharing

You can also save backup files to your phone's storage, and share receipts or reports through apps you choose (for example WhatsApp). These go only where you send them.

4. License activation

To activate a license, the app shows a Device code that you send to the developer yourself (for example by WhatsApp). The Device code is created from a random key made inside your phone; it does not contain your phone number, IMEI, name or business data. The developer keeps your name, shop name, phone number (if you give it) and Device code to issue and support your license. Activation itself works offline.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

6. Permissions

  • Internet – only for Google Drive backup and restore.
  • Notifications – to tell you when a backup finished or needs attention.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it from the phone. Backups in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them as described above. To have your license details removed from the developer's records, contact us.

8. Security

The app's database and its backups are encrypted, and the app is locked with a PIN or fingerprint. Please keep your PIN private and your phone and Google account secure.

9. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
