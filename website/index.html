EggVyapar

Egg business manager for Android, in English and Hindi

EggVyapar helps egg traders, wholesalers and shops keep their daily business in one place on their phone: who bought how many eggs, who paid, who still owes money, and how the business is doing.

What you can do

  • Customers and suppliers with their balances and full history
  • Sales and purchases (trays and eggs), with payment taken on the spot
  • Payments received and paid, and outstanding dues
  • Egg stock, rates and expenses
  • Receipts to share with customers
  • Dashboard and reports for any day, week or month
  • Backups to a file on the phone or to the user's own Google Drive
  • Works offline; the business data is stored encrypted on the phone and protected by a PIN or fingerprint

Google Drive backup

If the user chooses to connect Google Drive, EggVyapar stores its encrypted backup files in the app's own hidden folder in that user's Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). EggVyapar cannot see or change any other files in the user's Drive. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Getting the app and a license

EggVyapar is sold with a lifetime license for each phone. To buy it or get help, contact the developer.
